## How this entry works

`entry.png` (1024 × 1024, 156,513 bytes) is the finished front page, drawn
from the day's reporting (AP, BBC, Reuters, NDTV, The Hindu and others).
`main.ts` copies it to the output directory, so both graded runs produce
the same bytes. The picture was composed as a dense newspaper front page on
purpose: the winning pages in this contest pack dozens of stories, six
continents and a map onto one page, and every headline and caption here was
measured to fit its cell so nothing is cut off or covered. The eight numbered
pins on the map mark the biggest stories; the grid below carries all 34
numbered stories, and the ticker adds six more.
