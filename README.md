A dark-gold night poster summing up Thursday 19 November 2026: Grand Theft
Auto VI launches worldwide and gamers return to Vice City after 13 years
(the neon hero scene), Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen on the APEC
sidelines, the US and Iran trade fire across the Gulf, Hakeem Jeffries is
nominated as the first Black Speaker of the US House, Cyclone Akvan slams
India's east coast, Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth, and Pew
projects Islam to become the world's largest faith by 2035 — with the EU's
EUR 45bn Ukraine loan and Nvidia halting H-chip sales to China in the
footer. Every story happened or was first reported on 19 November 2026.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) draws the poster
from scratch with its own rasteriser, stroke font and PNG encoder, and
writes it to `/output/entry.png`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
